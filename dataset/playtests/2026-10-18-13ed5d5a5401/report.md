## Fixes

1. Never name the culprit in a public round reveal. Round 3 should say the backup log contradicts the operator's book and lists a handset number, and let players read the number themselves.
2. Hard rule: NPCs and GM answers may only quote what a player actually typed out loud. No invented alibis or testimony for player characters ("Rishabh says he was by the stove", "Himanshu says he heard the outer door").
3. Whispers and NPC lines must not report another player's private actions unless an NPC plainly witnessed them in-world; strip "Nikhil was kneeling at the safe" style lines.
4. Score out-loud moves for every player, including clue shares and confession moments. Log them as moves so a player who speaks up is never paid zero for the biggest beat of the game.
5. Punish out-of-world talk with points only. Do not also make the world fail. Public knowledge like the diary code must work for whoever tries it.
6. Answer the in-world half of a mixed message. Dock the point for the sway, then still resolve the legitimate question or action.
7. Refund the action when the engine refuses it ("clue already visible", "no vote right now"). Tell the player why and let them act again that round.
8. Raise or disclose the +3 play cap; two players lost 3 and 4 earned points to a silent ceiling.
9. Stop posting "X stayed quiet, it cost an override" for players who acted that round; check acted-this-round before charging or announcing it.
