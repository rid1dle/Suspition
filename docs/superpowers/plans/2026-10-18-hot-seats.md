# Hot Seats Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make the room the game. Hot seats put one player on the spot with GM-written questions and a whisper, every card opens on a playable hook, Point is free, a spotlight vote reveals instead of removes, private answers are short, and round events are loud.

**Architecture:**
- **Hot seats** are game state with a tick, in `server/game.ts`, like moments and votes. One background GM call per round writes them; if it fails, a fallback built from the cards still runs the seats.
- **Hooks, distrust and reveal lines** come from the card-check call that already runs after the world is written (`AUDIT_SCHEMA` / `applyAudit`). That adds no GM call.
- **Screens** reuse the table and phone components.

**Tech Stack:** Node 24 TypeScript, React (Next 16), node:test via tsx.

**Spec:** `docs/designs/hot-seats.md` (approved). Its "How it plays" section holds the numbers.

## Global Constraints

- **Seat count:** seats per round = ceil(players ÷ planned rounds), at least 1, at most 3.
- **Seat timing:** seat 1 gets ready 60 s after the round starts. Later seats are spaced evenly through the round timer. Each seat has a 15 s get-ready with the whisper shown, then a 90 s live timer.
- **Askers:** at most 3 per seat, rotating so every non-seated player asks during the round. Questions are at most 15 words.
- **Whisper:** at most 3 lines, each at most 12 words.
- **Follow-ups:** conditional lines only ("If X says Y, tell them: Z"). No taps and no typed text are needed, so player text never reaches the seat writer. The reader wall is unchanged.
- **Clue pacing:** seats in round index i of n may only touch side secrets until i ≥ n − 2. From then on, facts pointing at the culprit or method are allowed.
- **Fallback:** if the seat call fails or times out (60 s, one retry), seats run with alibi questions ("Where were you at {time}?"). The whisper is the player's hook line, and there are no follow-ups.
- **Card:**
  - Always shown: side, hook, distrust, alibi, real night.
  - "What drives you" (wants, fears) unlocks after your first hot seat.
  - "Why you did what you did" (reasons) unlocks after your second seat, or when you top a spotlight vote.
  - Locked content is never sent to the phone.
- **Point:**
  - free, one tap, banner "{A} suspects {B}!" with a 20 s defense timer
  - a 60 s cooldown per player
  - not allowed during a hot seat
  - one banner at a time, at most 2 waiting
- **Table priority:** loud event, then vote reveal, then hot seat, then Point.
- **Room setting `roundStyle`:** `'hotseats'` (the default) or `'classic'`.
  - Classic keeps today's round question and replays.
  - Hot seats replaces the round question with the spotlight vote.
- **Spotlight vote:**
  - no self-votes, secret votes revealed together
  - a tie gives no reveal ("The room is split.")
  - the most-voted player's next reveal line goes public; when the lines run out, the table says "Nothing new about {name}."
  - a vote on a non-culprit lowers suspicion by 10
- **Private answers:** at most 2 lines, each ending with a room action. The default private questions per round becomes 2 in hot seats style.
- **Words:** plain simple English, no em dashes.
- **Tests:** never modify an existing test without asking. New tests go in `test/hot-seats.test.ts` and `test/hot-seats-ui.test.tsx`.

## Review Focus

1. **3 players and 3 rounds:** 1 seat a round, 2 askers each. The rotation must not seat the same player twice before everyone has sat once. Test in Task 2.
2. **The host ends a round before its seats ran:** the unrun players go first next round, and nothing is left "live". Test in Task 2.
3. **The seat call answers after its round already ended:** the output for the old round is ignored. Test in Task 3.
4. **A locked card section leaks through the view** (for example, reasons in the phone JSON). Test in Task 1.
5. **Point spam:** a cooldown, and no Point during a seat. Test in Task 5.

**Interfaces:**

  - `unlockCard(game, pid, section)`.
  - Scenario players carry `reveals: string[]`. These are never sent to any phone.
- **Audit prompt rules:**
  - The hook is one line in the second person: who you are and what you want tonight.
  - `distrusts` must be another player's id, spread across players where the count allows.

### Task 2: Hot seat engine

**Files:**
- Modify:
  - `server/game.ts`: new seat state and functions, called from `startPlay`, `completePhase` and `beginPhaseChange`.
  - `server/app.ts`: `tick` calls `tickSeats`; host `seatNow`.
  - `server/index.ts`: `POST /api/seatnow`.
- Test: `test/hot-seats.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `seatsPerRound(players: number, rounds: number): number`
  - `planSeats(game, now)`: at round start. Sets `st.seats = { round, queue: string[], list: Seat[] }`.
    - `Seat = { pid, status: 'waiting'|'getready'|'live'|'done', readyAt, liveAt, endsAt, askers: {pid, question, followup}[], whisper: string[], written: boolean }`.
    - A new game's queue is a fair rotation in an order the server shuffles once. Ruling: the GM is not called to order it.
  - `tickSeats(game, now): boolean`: waiting→getready at `readyAt`, getready→live at `readyAt + 15 s`, live→done at `liveAt + 90 s`. Done calls `unlockCard`.
  - `seatNow(game, now)`: the next waiting seat becomes getready now.
  - `askersFor(game, round, seatIndex): string[]`: at most 3, rotating.
- **Constants:** `SEAT_READY_MS = 15_000`, `SEAT_LIVE_MS = 90_000`, `SEAT_FIRST_MS = 60_000`.

- Test: `test/hot-seats.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- `SEATS_SCHEMA = { seats: [{ player_id, whisper: string[], askers: [{ player_id, question, followup }] }] }`
- `seatsPrompt` gets:
  - the round's seats and askers;
  - each seated player's card truth;

- `applySeatWriting` ignores output for a round that is no longer current, and caps the lengths (whisper 3×12 words, question 15 words, follow-up 25 words).
- A follow-up must start with "If ". Anything else is dropped.
