# UI review against the UI rules, 2026-10-19

A walk through every screen of a seeded test world ("Blood in the Tasting Room", 4 players), the table at 1440 x 900 and a phone at 400 x 860, checked against `docs/ui-rules.md`, `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md` and impeccable's laws. Screens: setup, briefing, playing, round vote, meeting, final vote, reveal, the phone chat and drawers.

## Fix now (all done, checked on screenshots)

1. **Banned side stripes** (impeccable absolute ban): the character card sections (public, secret, never knew) and the reveal's secret and "they never knew" boxes use a thick coloured left border. Use a tinted surface and a coloured label instead.
2. **Colours that ignore the world:** right answers in fixed emerald, hidden facts and whispers in fixed violet, public sections in fixed sky, white text on the danger colour. Take them from the world's palette (mixed with a fixed hue so the meaning holds), so every world stays readable and in its own colours.
3. **The reveal is a wall:** the truth timeline runs about 40 lines in one column while the other column sits half empty. Lead with the story and the cards; fold the step-by-step night under "The night, step by step".
4. **Empty panels shown:** "Rounds: No round votes", "How people found out: The GM people learned nothing new", "Shared clues: none yet" on the final screens. Fewest options holds for panels too: hide a panel with nothing in it.
5. **The briefing table screen wastes the space:** a short briefing card sits over an empty left column while the right column runs a long alibi table. Put the room's readiness and "Start round 1" under the briefing, and fold the alibis.
6. **The table's top bar says the world name twice** when the GM names round 1 after the world (a small line and again in the round title).

(Two things that looked wrong in full-page screenshots were not: the phone header sits at the top and the table's bottom bar leaves room under it, in a real browser window.)
